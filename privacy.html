Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

PitchPlan ("we," "us," "the app") is a free tool that helps youth soccer coaches build age-appropriate practice plans and share home practice with players and parents. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version: Coaches create an account with an email and password. Parents and players never create an account — they access their team's homework using a join code, with no personal information collected from them at all.

Information Coaches Provide

  • Account information: your email address and password, used to sign in (handled securely by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see or store your password ourselves).
  • Content you create: team names, age groups, practice plans, and any custom drills you add.
  • A basic profile answer: whether you've coached before, used only to simplify the app's interface for first-time coaches.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect any personal information from children. Players are never asked to create an account, provide a name tied to an identity, an email, or a photo.
  • Parents/guardians access their team's homework via a join code and are never required to enter personal information to view or use it.
  • When a completed drill is logged, it is recorded anonymously (tied to the team, not to any individual person).

How We Use Information

Coach account and content data is used solely to operate the app: to show you your teams, plans, and drill library, and to let parents view homework you've assigned. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where Data Is Stored

App data is stored using Google Firebase, a cloud infrastructure provider. Data may be processed on servers located outside your country.

Local Device Storage

The app may store a small amount of information directly in your browser (such as a light/dark mode preference) using local storage. This never leaves your device and is not collected by us.

Children's Privacy

PitchPlan is designed with children's privacy as a priority. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. The parent/player experience is intentionally built to require no account and no personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us in a way not described here, please contact us and we will remove it.

Data Retention and Deletion

Coach account data and content are retained until you request deletion. To request that your account and associated data be deleted, contact us using the information below.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. We'll update the "Last updated" date above when we do.
